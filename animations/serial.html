How it works: A single thread executes the nested loops over (y, x). Every cell at timestep n+1 is computed sequentially — cell (0,0), then (0,1), then (0,2)… After all 100 cells finish, the time loop advances and the sweep starts again. No parallelism. Total work = 100 cells × number of timesteps, all done by one CPU core.
